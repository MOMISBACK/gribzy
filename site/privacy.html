Privacy policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026 · Last updated: July 29, 2026

Gribzy is designed to work without an account and to keep your GRIB weather files on your device. This policy explains what the Gribzy Android application and website process.

Summary

  • No Gribzy account is required.
  • No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK is included.
  • GRIB files, preferences and the local library remain in the application’s private storage.
  • Location is used only when you request it to center a weather area.

Data processed by the application

Local weather files and preferences

Files you import or download, their technical metadata, names, selected areas and language preference are stored locally in the application’s private document storage. Gribzy does not automatically upload these files or diagnostics to a Gribzy server.

Location

If you grant foreground location permission, Gribzy reads your approximate or precise device location to display it and center the area you choose. Location is not used in the background, is not retained as a location history and is not sent to a Gribzy server. The coordinates of the selected map area are sent to NOAA only when you request a GFS forecast download.

Network and map requests

When online features are used, the application contacts NOAA NOMADS to download GFS weather data and OpenFreeMap to display online map tiles. These providers receive normal network information such as your IP address and request details under their own privacy policies. Gribzy does not control their independent logs.

Feedback and beta forms

The application and website may open Google Forms when you voluntarily report a bug, request a feature or apply for beta testing. Nothing is submitted automatically. Google processes the information you choose to enter under Google’s own privacy terms. Do not include a GRIB file, precise location or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and you intend to share it.

Website data

The Gribzy website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral analytics. It stores only the language you select in your browser’s local storage. The hosting provider may maintain standard security and access logs, including IP address, user agent and request time.

Retention, deletion and security

Local data remains on your device until you delete a dataset, clear the application’s storage or uninstall Gribzy. You can revoke location permission at any time in Android settings. Gribzy uses the operating system’s private application storage, but no storage or transmission method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

Children, sale of data and legal requests

Gribzy is a general outdoor weather tool and is not directed to children. Gribzy does not sell personal data. Because no Gribzy account or cloud profile is created, Gribzy normally has no account data to access, correct or delete. Information submitted through Google Forms can be addressed through the contact method below.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Gribzy’s features or providers change. The current version and its update date will remain available on this page. For a privacy question, deletion request concerning feedback, bug report or missing feature, use the Gribzy feedback form.
